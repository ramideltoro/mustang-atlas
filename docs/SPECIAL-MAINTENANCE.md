## 3.7L V6 head-gasket project

`/#/maintenance/3-7l-v6-head-gasket` provides original enthusiast explanations in 39 numbered steps across six named phases, preparation tables, bank-specific branches, diagnostic decisions, completion checks and 29 workshop references. Its scope is the supplied **2015 U.S. Mustang 3.7L** collection with factory-style head bolts. Do not broaden it to later years, transverse installations or aftermarket studs without verifying their own documentation.

The two cylinder-head procedures are `G1681515` (LH) and `G1681516` (RH), section 303-01B, carrying a September 16, 2022 revision in the supplied collection. Their source hashes and those of all dependent procedures are stored privately in the content record. Stale sources withhold the guide through the existing catalog validator.

The guide now writes out the dependent access and reassembly operations: intake, coils, both valve covers, front accessories, timing drive, camshafts and remaining head attachments. Steps 19–24 explicitly cover lifting the head, removing the old gasket, cleaning/inspection, placing the new gasket and tightening the head. The original release was too dependent on component links to function as the requested walkthrough. Bank-specific location, sequence, belt-routing and tool-seating illustrations remain required linked references. No unsupported shortcut or labor-time estimate is supplied.

Direct section links use `?section=access-head-gasket` and `?section=replace-head-gasket` after the guide route. Group validation requires consecutive coverage of every step without overlaps, gaps or duplicate IDs. Existing ungrouped guides retain their usual layout. The mobile section menu collapses, and numbered steps use the available width.

### Reference decisions

Four checked public links accompany the article: Ford's 2015 3.7L capacities/specifications, Fel-Pro's torque-to-yield head-bolt explanation, its surface-finish explanation, and its sealer video page. These are conceptual/owner references, not alternate torque specifications. Optional public reference records require HTTPS and reject credentials or executable URLs.

The supplied block-flatness table has inconsistent metric/imperial pairs. The article flags the discrepancy and requires the current Ford limit before accepting the block; it does not pick or invent a corrected limit. It also distinguishes the owner's older oil specification from the later head-procedure revision and preserves the unusual explicit oil-filter seal instruction.

Codex authorship and reference checks do not claim hands-on execution or professional certification. Source figure geometry and technical instructions should receive qualified review before broadening coverage.
