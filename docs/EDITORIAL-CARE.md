# Care guide editorial revision

The 45 legacy care guides are being rewritten individually. `wheel-hubs`, `3-7l-v6-head-gasket`, and `3-7l-v6-timing-chain` are intentionally excluded because their detailed upgrades already exist.

Run `node scripts/care-editorial-author.mjs --ids cabin-filter` for a bounded pilot, or omit `--ids` to process the full queue. `--limit N` bounds the selected queue. The worker uses one signed-in Codex CLI process at a time: author, independent technical reviewer, and up to three repair/re-review passes when required. API-key environment variables are stripped; no model API calls are made. Accepted records are resumed by exact evidence fingerprint. Do not launch two instances concurrently.

Inputs are the complete text of each guide's primary source documents, structured tables/nested actions, selected direct repair/specification dependencies, visually supplied primary illustrations, hash-bound reviewed evidence supplements, and official Ford owner references. Owner pages are retained as complete `.ClassContentBody` text with table separators in `work/editorial-care/evidence`. Primary source illustrations not visually supplied are explicitly identified; the author may not infer their details. Unresolved essential instructions cause a held record, not fabricated coverage.

The author must teach the promised task: preparation, access, ordered actions, what to observe, interpretation, next decisions, reassembly, and verification. References accompany the relevant instructions. Engine and transmission branches remain explicit. The voice is original, friendly, funny, and occasionally mildly vulgar, with clear serious warnings and specifications. Editorial jokes never substitute for instructions or invent repair memories.

`work/editorial-care/accepted/<id>.json` contains the proposed replacement guide, source fingerprint, topic coverage, limitations, and independent technical assessment. Nothing automatically changes the published content catalogs. Raw input, author output, reviewer findings, revisions, logs, and any held reason remain in `work/editorial-care/jobs/<id>/`. These files contain private source excerpts and are not public site artifacts.

Run `node scripts/care-editorial-validate.mjs` to test a temporary merged catalog against the production validator and check immutable applicability, schedules, evidence IDs, related links, categories, and rules. Review `validation.json`, accepted samples, and all held records before merging. The automated independent review is not human or professional mechanic review. `progress.json` and the accepted directory provide honest counts; do not claim all guides revised while records remain pending.

The worker has a PID lock. Create `work/editorial-care/stop.request` to stop after the current guide; remove it before resuming. Authentication or usage-limit errors stop the queue for attention rather than spending repeated failed calls. A merged accepted guide can resume without being rewritten merely because the public catalog now contains its new prose.

Oil-service evidence includes the three base-engine oil-pan procedures (only their isolated oil-service operations are relevant), engine specifications, lifting/access documentation, and exact drain/filter/shield illustration inputs. Official owner capacities and workshop specifications disagree for the 2015 2.3L refill quantity; the worker must hold that unresolved branch rather than silently choosing a number. Spark-plug evidence explicitly includes the 3.7L upper intake and outlet pipe, quick-release coupling procedures, battery/window requirements, and engine ignition specifications so removing the coils is not an unexplained shortcut. Relevant lifting and safety overview dependencies are included for other guides.

`node scripts/care-editorial-owner-evidence.mjs` bootstraps all owner references from the maintenance catalog and refreshes the official parts and three engine-specification articles discovered from the verified 2015 Ford owner navigation. Structured table row/column spans are retained alongside article text. This fetches public source evidence only; it does not call a model API.
